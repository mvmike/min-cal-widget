A Minimal Calendar Widget that's always on current date

<b>Features</b>

* Free and open source
* Contains no ads or payments
* Two formats: Month and Week (resizable with initial respective values of 3x2 and 3x1)
* Displays all events from your calendars with a symbol set (high number becomes generic)
* Native widget configuration access or press widget’s top right corner
* Pressing on any other part of the widget opens calendar application
* Highly configurable: start day of the week, theme, symbol set, symbol set colour, transparency, text size, show week number...
